AI MANUFACTURING DIGITAL TWIN
PHASE 11 DIGITAL TWIN STATE ENGINE
======================================================================

Samples: 131

The Digital Twin combines:
1. Age-aware lifecycle health
2. Current/context condition health
3. Isolation Forest anomaly score
4. SHAP degradation drivers

Health-gap interpretation:
Condition health substantially below lifecycle health may indicate unexpected/accelerated degradation.
Condition health substantially above lifecycle health indicates the observed condition looks healthier than its lifecycle estimate.
